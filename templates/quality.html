Quality assurance

We follow strict quality control measures at every step to ensure our products meet international standards.

Quality is Our Promise

Tested at every stage

From raw material intake to finished product, every batch passes through documented checkpoints.

01
Raw Material Inspection

Incoming botanicals are checked for identity, moisture, and contamination before acceptance.

02
In-Process Testing

Samples are drawn at drying, milling, and extraction stages to track consistency.

03
Finished Product Testing

Every batch is tested for microbial load, heavy metals, and active compound content.

04
Microbial Analysis

Total plate count, yeast and mold, E. coli, and Salmonella screening on every lot.

05
Heavy Metal Analysis

Lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury levels are verified against international limits.

06
Stability Testing

Shelf-life studies confirm your ingredient performs from shipment to formulation.
